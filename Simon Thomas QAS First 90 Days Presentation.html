When to become involved and when to step back

Observe, ask, offer, act

Ask me about
Observe
Ask
Offer
Act

Some people need ownership. Others need clarity, or a responsibility taken off them, so they can act.

I step back from

  • Daily coaching and practitioner work
  • Decisions inside the agreed plan
  • Anywhere my voice only adds noise

I step in on

  • Athlete welfare, safety or integrity
  • Investment not serving the plan
  • QAS and the NSO pulling apart
  • Barriers beyond the sport's reach
